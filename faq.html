Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Patevan, privacy, features, and how everything works.

What is Patevan?

Patevan is a friendly AI companion designed to help with everyday conversations and tasks while respecting your privacy. Unlike many AI assistants, Patevan is built to work primarily on your device rather than relying on the cloud for everything.

Is Patevan free?

Patevan is a one-time purchase of $9.99. There are no required subscriptions or monthly fees.

Does Patevan require an account?

No. Patevan does not require you to create an account to use the app.

Does Patevan collect my personal information?

No. Patevan is designed with privacy in mind. Your conversations and personal information stay on your device unless you choose to use an online feature.

Does Patevan work offline?

Yes. Most of Patevan’s core features work completely offline using an on-device AI model.

Some features, such as weather, require an internet connection.

Why does Patevan take a moment to start?

When Patevan first launches, he loads his offline AI brain into memory. This usually takes a few seconds depending on your device. Once loaded, he’s ready to chat.

What phones does Patevan support?

Patevan is designed for modern Android phones running Android 13 or newer. Faster processors and additional RAM provide the best experience.

Can Patevan recognize people or objects?

If you give Patevan camera permission, he can recognize familiar faces you’ve taught him and describe what he sees around him.

Does Patevan remember things I teach him?

Yes. If you ask Patevan to remember something, he saves it locally on your device so he can recall it later.

Is my information sent to the cloud?

Not for Patevan’s offline features. Online features such as weather temporarily connects to online services only when needed.

Can I use Patevan without internet?

Yes. Patevan was designed so you can continue using many of his most important features even without Wi-Fi or mobile data.

How do I update Patevan?

Updates are delivered through the Google Play Store just like any other Android app.

Can I support the project?

Yes. Optional one-time support purchases are available inside the app. They are never required and do not unlock any core features.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please visit the Get Help page and send us a message with as much detail as possible — including your device model, Android version, Patevan version, and what happened.

Is Patevan still improving?

Absolutely. Patevan is an active project. New features, improvements, and fixes will continue to be added over time based on user feedback.

Who created Patevan?

Patevan was created by Patrick Lippy, an independent developer, as a passion project with the goal of building a friendly, private, and affordable AI companion that anyone can own without expensive hardware or recurring subscriptions.

Still Have a Question?

We’re happy to help.

If your question wasn’t answered here, visit our Get Help page and send us a message. The more details you can provide, the easier it will be for us to help.

Thank you for being part of Patevan’s journey. Your feedback, ideas, and bug reports help make Patevan better for everyone.
